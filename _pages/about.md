I am a researcher and engineer from Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research centers on **computer vision**, **multimodal AI**, and **agentic AI**, with a growing interest in efficient perception for real-world systems.

My work so far has explored how machines interpret visual information and use it to act, from [inferring the mood of a scene from its visual attributes](/publication/2021-music-suggestions-image-atmosphere) (IEEE ICCIT 2021) to [a LangChain-based conversational agent that selects between image-captioning and object-detection tools](/publication/2025-langchain-image-conversational-ai) to answer questions about images (IEEE MPSec ICETA 2025). More recently, I have been exploring real-time human pose estimation with efficient vision transformers.

Alongside my full-time role as an [Assistant Lead Engineer (QA)](/professional-experience/) at bKash Limited, I am a remote [Research Assistant](/research-teaching/) at ELITE Research Lab, working on agentic AI and multi-agent systems. I hold a [B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering](/education/) from American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB) with a CGPA of 3.99/4.00, graduating *Summa Cum Laude* with the university Gold Medal.

In the long term, I hope to build efficient, reliable vision and multimodal agents that not only understand what they see but can act on it dependably under real-world constraints.
